Pumpkin Mac&Cheese

This version of Mac and Cheese is the perfect healthy comfort food. The recipe is easy and quick to make. Ideal for cozy season.

Ingredients

  • 100g Cashews
  • 245g Pumpkin Purée
  • 3 Garlic Cloves
  • 300g Butternut Squash
  • 340g Macaroni
  • 1l Water
  • 100g Breadcrumbs
  • 4tbsp Oatmeal
  • 2tbsp Olive Oil

Instructions

  1. Soak the cashews for at least one hour in water.
  2. Preheat the oven to 220°C
  3. Rinse the cashews and blend together with puree, oats garlic, water and olive oil. Season with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  4. Peel and dice the squash. Combine the squash with the macaroni in a casserole dish
  5. Add the sauce and mix well.
  6. Cover with tin foil and bake for 45 minutes.
  7. Remove tin foil, mix again and top with breadcrumbs. Bake for another 10 Minutes
